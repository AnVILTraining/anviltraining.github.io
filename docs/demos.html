AnVIL Demos

AnVIL Demos are a monthly, virtual meeting where we highlight what you can do on AnVIL! AnVIL Demos start with a 30-minute demonstration which can cover a range of topics, from a capability of the platform to a scientific analysis powered by AnVIL.

After the demonstration, we’ll open up the floor to answer questions about the demo as well as any general questions you might have about AnVIL.

View Past Demos

Upcoming Demos

Announcements about upcoming AnVIL Demos will be posted on our community forum. After the Demo, the post will be updated with a link to a recording and a summary of the Q&As. Check out the latest topic for information about our next Demo, or browse old topics to view previous Demos that interest you.

Sign up for future Demos

You can also sign up to hear about future AnVIL Demos as well as general AnVIL announcements at bit.ly/anvil-mailing-list

We’d love to showcase how you use AnVIL! If you’re interested in presenting at a future AnVIL Demos session, reach out to Natalie Kucher (nkucher3@jhu.edu).
